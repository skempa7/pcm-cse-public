# Printable cases and case expansion - September 14, 2026

The public application now has 34 fictional presentations and 82 playable case/variant paths. **Cases & print** is the main navigation entry. Choose a presentation and its path, then choose only the material needed.

## Materials

- Doorway information: supplied information only, portrait.
- Patient script: first-person statements, HPI first, then surgical history, medications, allergies and reactions, social history, family history, past medical history, and review of systems. Landscape, two columns, bold topics and page references. One opening/reveal instruction replaces repeated question aliases and teaching paragraphs.
- Examinations and findings: compact system-grouped examination/finding rows. Conditional additions are distinguished. Findings are released only after the relevant action is performed or explicitly simulated.
- Blank SOAP workspace: two portrait pages with useful writing space and evenly spaced rules.
- Example SOAP: a clinical note for the complete demonstrated encounter. Course/authoring caveats sit outside the note. The online and printed example use the same reviewed data.
- Student copy: doorway plus blank SOAP, no answers.
- Complete practice packet: all five sections, each starting on a new page.

Student materials use a restricted supplied-information route. They do not fetch teaching answers, change assistance status or add examination evidence. Answer-bearing previews preserve the existing explicit conversion requirement during independent/exam-rehearsal attempts. Print access is checked again before printing. Delayed requests cannot overwrite another case, reopen a closed preview or clear a newer preview.

The preceding PARTNER-PRINT-2026-09-13.md describes the superseded, longer script format. These documents remain generated from the canonical case and demonstrated encounter; there is no separately maintained PDF fact bank.

## Verification and limits

Verification covers all 82 paths and all seven print editions: 574 actual PDF exports. Checks separately measure printed source inclusion, page geometry, atomic question/answer and exam/finding blocks, source/build identity, and access boundaries. Rendered visual review checks typography, tables, writing space and page breaks; it is not inferred from a successful text extraction.

Browser checks exercise Cases & print navigation, search/system filtering, safe and protected previews, explicit conversion/cancellation, printing, title/focus/scroll restoration, narrow-window reflow, stylesheet-load recovery, slow/failing access checks, and four navigation races. Online/printed note agreement is checked for a new case, a revised case and both types of legacy authoring caveat. Disposable visible practice walkthroughs exercise new case selection, out-of-order questions, performed examinations and the resulting Notes entries.

All ten new cases and 13 modified existing presentations received individual source/clinical-content review, including 49 resolved paths. This is not faculty or clinician approval. Existing library-wide checks establish internal consistency and evidence boundaries; they do not independently validate every clinical proposition in all older cases.

Three legacy presentations still lack a supported third differential: watery diarrhea, distal neuropathy and recurrent headache. The unsupported alternatives are omitted from the normal online/print answer key and the gap is explicitly marked. Three other corrected alternatives need course-category review: right-upper-quadrant pain, acute retention and lower-urinary-tract symptoms. Shared or unresolved VINDICATE categories are flagged rather than receiving invented category credit.

The symbolic auditor does not recognize all reasonable wording; unrecognized claims remain unscored rather than being silently credited. Case-specific skin, ear, nose and throat appearances are described in labeled examination results; the generic 3D model is not claimed to display them accurately. Voice, course timing, grading rules and the separate unresolved opening-evidence policy were not redesigned in this pass.

Only the public application is current. No private course PDFs, real encounter databases, paid providers, API credentials or analytics were added to the public package. Verification artifacts and example PDF copies remain local under ignored verification-private/printables-20260914/.
