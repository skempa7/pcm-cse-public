# User-supplied room and textures

Geometry: InteriorTest.blend, supplied by the app owner as 30-livingroom_blender.rar. Alternate OBJ/FBX archives contain the same InteriorTest scene. Author/source/license were not included in these archives and have not been independently established. This application does not grant a redistribution license for those assets.

Wood textures: supplied folder “living room ibra1991 01 July 2017 11 25 pm”, from https://free3d.com/3d-model/living-room-3728.html by ibra1991. That page lists Personal Use License. The owner explicitly requested the combined asset in both private and free public educational versions. Attribution does not expand that license; these assets are not CC0. Editable third-party source is retained locally and omitted from this public repository.

The app adds its own examination table and original US flag. Existing Poly Haven fabric/lighting remain CC0 under https://polyhaven.com/license .
