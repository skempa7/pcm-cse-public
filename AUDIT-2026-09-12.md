# Independent public-app review — September 12, 2026

Review baseline: `88afd0fe4de15f6ca1488d4ba8efdf4afc7e7221`. Resulting app and engine version: **4.0.4**. This review concerns the current `pcm-cse-public` repository, its browser package, authored case library, teaching materials, and shipped 3D assets. Earlier dated reports describe earlier snapshots.

## Scope and established decisions

The review compared the actual source and commit changes with the relevant user requests and Claude conversation, rather than accepting completion summaries. The public application is the only current edition. The intended workflow is doorway → Talk/Physical Exam/Notes → SOAP → evidence-grounded feedback, with written teaching and deliberate practice alongside it.

Established decisions preserved: fixed patient voices with an output toggle; no paid provider/backend; always-available orbit and compact camera controls; anatomical appearance toggle; original female hair and bald male with mustache; current body shapes and shirt artwork; intentionally retained clothing overlap; Home/save safeguards; OLDCARTS Notes; course-timed rehearsal and separate assisted practice. Private source documents, actual saved attempts, historical assets, and unrelated projects were not modified or published.

Current source/output roles and historical/ignored working material are described in README.md. The delivered renderer is `web/patient3d/dist/room.js`; the browser Python package is `web/engine.zip`. Asset-export scripts with historical local inputs are not interchangeable with the current manually refined runtime assets.

## Important defects corrected

### Dialogue and evidence

- Numeric normalization changed `2` and `4` into `to` and `for`. A summary saying two days could be confirmed after a four-day cough history. Numeric values are preserved, and each summary claim must match actually disclosed speech. Unrelated additions and unsupported diagnoses cannot be confirmed from pooled vocabulary.
- Questions about a relative could return the patient's own symptoms or onset. Family scope now stays within the corresponding authored family facts.
- Compound demographics and activity questions dropped later questions. Name/age/occupation and activity plus another symptom question now retain their separate asks.
- Common ROS phrasings such as “Any shortness of breath?” missed existing authored responses. Narrow matching now reaches those facts without manufacturing negatives for unauthored symptoms.
- Opening-history evidence could combine unrelated clauses, reverse pain migration, or be credited under an unrelated history heading. The matching now preserves clause and section scope.
- Findings such as “No murmurs, rubs or gallops” could wrongly support a positive fragment. Inherited list negation is preserved and stops at a full sentence boundary.
- Literal qualitative reflex findings and simple occupation/household phrases were incorrectly left unrecognized. Exact delivered wording can now be accepted without inferring synonyms, changing relatives or dropping clinical qualifiers.
- An explicit ROS denial could be correctly earned but omitted from Notes. Notes now projects the actual answer under its directly asked, authorized concept, with repeated answers deduplicated.

### Reliability and usability

- Retrying the microphone through its error message did not restore the hands-free flag. Retry now follows the same lifecycle as the main control. Escape cancels buffered recognition, stale final events cannot submit after cancellation, and cumulative results are not replayed after a patient interruption.
- A failed question could disappear or overwrite a newer draft. Typed and dictated failed text is retained together with newer composer text; it is not automatically replayed because the original action may already have happened.
- A browser-storage failure permanently poisoned the worker until reload, risking unsaved in-memory work. Recovery now retries only the pending storage flush before allowing further operations, without repeating the original action.
- Delayed browser voice availability could queue multiple old replies or speech from a previous patient. Only the latest eligible reply can retry, cancellation removes it, and synchronous playback failure releases the suppressed microphone.
- The exam board collapsed in short windows while a finding was visible. Short desktop panels now scroll as one unit, and stacked mobile layouts reserve usable space for actions. Visual review caught a remaining flex-ancestor clip that size-only checks missed; the parent layout was corrected and the browser check now verifies real click targets.

### Patient view, teaching and release

- Full-patient framing cropped lying-down patients in narrow panels. It now fits actual posed patient bounds to the panel aspect ratio and handles resize while preserving a manually adjusted orbit.
- Three female prone assets intersected their support cushion. A small local cushion compression restores clearance without altering the patient's body shape or the requested clothing fit.
- Exam-guide normal documentation examples were too easy to confuse with obtained findings. They are now explicitly conditional rehearsal examples; guide completion creates no clinical evidence. Mobile wrapping, focus return and current control names were checked.
- A separate reading of all distinct unknown example statements found three stale claims: a roommate instead of the reported husband, an asserted previous-pneumonia denial instead of a similar-episode denial, and missed work instead of worry about missing work. The examples now follow the actual patient replies.
- Sixty-nine walkthroughs had stale case fingerprints. All 72 were replayed and regenerated against the current engine, dialogue and examination evidence. The generator validates the complete library before replacing outputs.
- Feedback had an incorrect explanation of Objective points and generic treatment/follow-up examples unsuitable for unrelated or urgent cases. These were corrected. Repair exercises use actual clinical evidence rather than social acknowledgments.
- Cache stamping previously missed nested worker, iframe and dynamically imported print-module references. Dependencies are now stamped before their containing files in one pass, with a read-only freshness check.
- README and third-party notices now describe the actual public application, limits and imported-room provenance, rather than retired editions or wholly self-created room assets.

## Verification and what it measures

Testing used disposable databases and isolated browser storage, not the user's saved attempts.

- **75 Python unit tests, 7,647 evidence safeguards and 9,064 Notes-condensation checks passed.** Dialogue/continuity, identity, question-generalization and actual Session checks also passed and exercise source behavior and regressions. The release route runner exercises all **72 case/variant paths**, including saved notes, immutable submissions and assistance gates.
- New dialogue regressions exercise **1,840 checks across 72 variants**. Existing patient chat has **2,989 checks**, continuity **984**, phrasing **674**, held-out generalization **365**, and Session conversations **59**. These establish the tested behavior, not universal language understanding.
- All **72 walkthroughs** replay **3,196 actual dialogue turns and 678 examination actions**, with current case/engine stamps and delivered-evidence links. Automated unknown classifications were separately reviewed for stale content; an unknown classification is not evidence that a sentence is accurate.
- Nine frontend test programs cover microphone lifecycle, hands-free speech, delayed voice events, error recovery, storage failure injection, toolbar behavior, reset controls, voice policy and guide structure. Storage tests verify that a failed mutation happens once and is not replayed on recovery.
- Actual Chrome UI checks cover microphone clicks and cancellation with a controlled recognizer; error recovery and draft reload; compound conversation; one-click examination and Notes notification; desktop, short-window and mobile layout; Home cancellation; SOAP save/reload/submission and feedback; written lesson printing; timed rehearsal and solution access; and duplicate-tab protection.
- Renderer checks use all four shipped body assets and four postures. There are **21 camera/framing/wardrobe tests**, actual mesh/table clearance checks, native drag interactions, reduced-motion checks, and missing-model failure/retry. Screenshots were visually inspected. Actual measured rendering was approximately **30 scene frames/second** on the review Mac; the engine's nominal counter was not used as proof of render throughput.
- The npm advisory service returned **zero affected package names** for the 30 dependency names/versions in the renderer lockfile at review time. This does not audit all vendored code or certify application security.
- Release checks compare the packaged engine with its sources, verify cache tags and the tracked-file manifest, and scan shipped text plus archive contents for known credentials, provider modules and paid endpoints.

The historical wardrobe test was updated because it asserted that every female anatomy mesh must be disabled while covered. Production intentionally uses a tiny dark subset to fill a trouser gap. Tests now require the bounded subset, authored vertices, untextured dark material and exact restoration of full anatomy/material. Male anatomy still must be disabled while clothed. No production wardrobe rule was loosened. Other test-harness fixes supply the actual current state/timer contract and remove duplicate runs of the same public directory; existing behavior assertions remain.

## Remaining limits

- Dialogue is authored and deterministic. Some free-form referral descriptions can still confuse a future specialist examination with another conversational intent; the written dysphagia example uses a verified equivalent formulation. A review probe set of 3,048 turns improved from 84.6% to 86.0% resolved, with no exceptions. Some remaining questions require context or request missing authored detail; unknown information must not be converted into a clinical denial to improve a coverage number.
- SOAP grading is provisional. Unrecognized example statements fell from **531 to 414** through exact-wording fixes and source corrections. Some legitimate concise paraphrases still receive no automatic credit. Evidence rules must stay strict; saved original grades are not silently recomputed under version 4.0.4.
- Automated consistency and source checks do not establish clinical accuracy of every synthetic case, diagnosis, treatment proposal, gesture, or anatomical surface.
- Hardware microphone/audio behavior, iOS/Android devices, every browser/OS combination, low-end GPU performance and physical realism were not established by this desktop review. Browser-vendor speech services remain subject to permission and network availability.
- The requested shirt/pants overlap and approximate tissue/cloth behavior remain. Imported room redistribution provenance remains unverified; no new permission or license is inferred from attribution.

This report separates source tests, browser interactions, visual inspection and source tracing. None of these individual checks substitutes for the others or establishes universal correctness.
